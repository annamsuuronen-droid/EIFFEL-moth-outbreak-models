## Predictor selection, variance and interpretation

The full-data predictive structure remains frozen: lag1 summer mean Tmin/mean Tmax/max Tmax,lag1 growing precipitation,lag1 autumn mean Tmax, plus NDVI mean/median/max at100m;1277 matched rows. Do not replace it with fold5's seven weather/two drought predictors or select a new baseline from outer scores.

Nested selections vary markedly: fold1 chooses no weather and NDVI at2000m; fold2 chooses11 weather/eight soil variables at2000m; folds3/4 choose three weather variables and no environment; fold5 chooses seven weather/two drought variables at100m. None selects the exact three full-data NDVI100m variables. Folds3/4's two labelled models have identical predictions because no environment was selected; these are legitimate ties, not independent supporting replications. `SELECTION_STABILITY.csv` documents all choices. Variation and weak held-out increments limit strong mechanism/scale claims; they do not authorize rerunning or altering frozen selection.

Predictive full-data variance proportions sum to1 for every species. Site contributes57.23%,59.65%,71.25%; year26.32%,25.82%,21.98%; weather14.41%,10.81%,1.81%; NDVI2.04%,3.72%,4.97% for autumnata/brumata/fagata. These are model-dependent in-sample latent variance allocations, not causal attribution or out-of-sample accuracy. Dominant site/year effects can absorb habitat differences, trap/exposure differences and unmeasured temporal processes. The joint model's individual environmental coefficient direction/uncertainty is not established by these group shares; no matching predictive coefficient/chain evidence is imported.

Retained code uses categorical site/year random levels with units, without coordinates, spatial covariance or autoregressive year structure. Thus it does not implement a spatial interpolation model or explicitly propagate outbreak cycles. Supplying all metadata units supports unseen-unit prediction mechanics; it does not validate unseen-site calibration. Weather lags are selected covariates, not an identified population-dynamics mechanism. Shuffled year folds include future calendar years in training. Fold2/3 candidates include current-year growing/autumn weather, and NDVI time/composite provenance is unresolved: no advance-forecast claim follows from this CV.

Legacy C+D fixed-predictor `cv_site` summaries do exist (environment-model SR2 approximately0.225/0.157/0.058 for autumnata/brumata/fagata). They use covariates selected on full data and full-panel scaling before partition prediction in the retained source. Retain them as exploratory summaries, not nested site validation; they cannot certify the whole selection procedure for new sites. Proper site/external validation is required for the spatial prediction now being assessed, with training-only selection and scaling and defined unseen-site/year effects. It remains deferred, not executed.

## Reproducibility and limitations

`tools/assess_model_readiness_v1.py --output <new directory>` performs only table parsing, hashes and arithmetic; it refuses an existing output directory and never loads RData/rds or model chains. Twelve source/audit transport identities,288 metrics,45 PPC flags,45 convergence-policy rows and166 frozen content fingerprints pass. CI includes nine transport/integrity tests, four diagnostic arithmetic tests, existing Windows safety suites and base-R checks. Input fingerprints are versioned; diagnostic outputs are new summaries derived from real existing exports. Primary raw observations, RDS posterior chains, modern convergence/trace diagnostics, six-species validation, honest new-site validation and advance/outbreak validation remain outside the available evidence. No missing quantity was imputed and no fit is declared converged solely from a completion marker or CV score.
